SLIDE 2 / WHAT THE 43 RECORDS REVEALED
Clock: 0:25-1:30. Total slide slot: 65 seconds.

Speaker A / Chi Kit WONG / 0:25-1:30

[SPOKEN]
The paper / Enabling Partial Participation in Remote Meetings introduced ProxyMe, a system for meeting updates and replies. We build on its partial participation problem. Our forty-three records add concrete needs: repeated requests when attention shifts, summaries missing personally relevant details, and a case where missing an important detail leads to a wrong answer. Some records also suggest that existing notes or chat already meet the need, without another tool. These examples motivate selective support. Our question is when to bring someone back, and what context helps them make a correct contribution.
[/SPOKEN]

SLIDE 4 / HOW AI SUPPORTS THE INTERACTION
Clock: 2:50-3:40. Total slide slot: 50 seconds.

Speaker B / Haodong XU / 2:50-3:40

[SPOKEN]
Meeting Cue runs in a browser beside Tencent Meeting. Browser speech recognition produces text. DeepSeek analyzes new text with the participant's role and availability, returning priority, source references, and a draft. The person checks the source, edits and copies the reply, then pastes and sends it in Tencent Meeting. AI can miss context, so checking and confirmation remain essential. Speech recognition and text analysis are separate services.
[/SPOKEN]
