Broad Survey on Partial Participation in Remote Meetings

预计填写时间 / Estimated time: 5–7 minutes

研究说明

Study Introduction

本问卷关注一种常见情境：当你因为另一场会议、课程或重要任务，无法完整参加某个远程会议时，你仍希望了解关键进展，并在真正需要你时短暂参与。我们把这种状态称为“部分参与”。这里的 AI 主要用于总结会议、筛选重要信息并在需要时提醒你，而不是自动替你做重要决定。

This survey studies a common situation: when another meeting, class, or important task prevents you from fully attending a remote meeting, you may still want to stay aware of key developments and re-engage briefly when your input is truly needed. We call this “partial participation.” In this survey, AI mainly summarizes the meeting, filters important information, and alerts you when needed; it is not assumed to autonomously make important decisions on your behalf.

所有回答仅用于课程研究分析，不涉及正确或错误答案。

All responses are for course research only. There are no right or wrong answers.
